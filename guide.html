🌱 Farming Guide

Learn the basic farming process for different crops.

Soybean

Important steps for growing soybean successfully.

  • Prepare the soil properly
  • Select good quality seeds
  • Sow during suitable season
  • Maintain proper moisture
  • Control weeds and pests
  • Harvest when mature

Cotton

Basic cultivation steps for cotton farming.

  • Prepare well-drained soil
  • Use quality cotton seeds
  • Maintain suitable spacing
  • Monitor irrigation
  • Control pests regularly
  • Harvest mature bolls

Wheat

Essential steps for wheat cultivation.

  • Prepare fine soil bed
  • Select healthy seeds
  • Sow at suitable time
  • Provide required irrigation
  • Manage weeds
  • Harvest after maturity

Rice

Basic information for rice cultivation.

  • Prepare and level the field
  • Use healthy seedlings
  • Maintain suitable water level
  • Apply nutrients as required
  • Control weeds and pests
  • Harvest mature crop

Maize

Important practices for maize farming.

  • Prepare fertile soil
  • Select quality seeds
  • Maintain proper plant spacing
  • Provide timely irrigation
  • Control weeds and pests
  • Harvest when grains mature

Onion

Basic cultivation information for onion.

  • Prepare loose soil
  • Use healthy seedlings
  • Maintain proper spacing
  • Provide regular irrigation
  • Control weeds
  • Harvest after bulb maturity
